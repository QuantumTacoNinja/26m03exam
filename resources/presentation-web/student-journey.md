## 01 · Inherit — Day 1 · 28 Sep

### Make your first safe change.

Trace a booking. Check a small fix. Explain the result.

- **Day 1 — 28 Sep — Meet the system**: Run the inherited application, trace a member's journey and use evidence to check a small maintenance change with a peer.

## 02 · Shape — Days 2–5 · 29 Sep–2 Oct

### Decide what belongs together.

Agree the language. Define the promises. Move one checked piece at a time.

- **Day 2 — 29 Sep — Find the boundaries**: Compare business meanings, build a shared glossary and explain a boundary with a counterexample.
- **Day 3 — 30 Sep — Make clear promises**: Agree service contracts. Separate responsibilities and check who is allowed to do what.
- **Day 4 — 1 Oct — Move safely**: Practise a versioned database migration, protect existing bookings and begin a small service extraction.
- **Day 5 — 2 Oct — Check independence**: Compare SQL with an object-relational mapper. Test data ownership, deployment and the complete member journey.

## 03 · Change — Days 6–10 · 5–9 Oct

### Turn a need into a checked change.

Test the connections. Agree safe change. Design a booking reschedule.

- **Day 6 — 5 Oct — Test the whole**: Run integration experiments. Compare the benefits and costs of your split. Agree a change policy.
- **Day 7 — 6 Oct — Practise recovery**: Exercise rollback, hand over to another operator and agree severity, responsibility and the on-call rota.
- **Day 8 — 7 Oct — Define the smallest useful change**: Agree what moving a booking to a differently priced slot must do, including permissions and uncertain outcomes.
- **Day 9 — 8 Oct — Choose what matters next**: Use operational evidence to explain causes and decide how to divide time between features, reliability and ongoing work.
- **Day 10 — 9 Oct — Review, implement, verify**: Review another team's specification. Implement a small part and check its behaviour with a peer.

## 04 · Operate — Days 11–13 · 12–14 Oct

### Own what happens after deployment.

Notice impact. Communicate. Recover. Make an evidence-based release decision.

- **Day 11 — 12 Oct — Make signals useful**: Connect useful logs and alerts to a responder. Check the response loop and a separate status page.
- **Day 12 — 13 Oct — Respond together**: Practise leading a response, containing impact, asking for help and checking recovery. Learn from the record.
- **Day 13 — 14 Oct — Release with evidence**: Check compatible versions and the complete rescheduling journey. Release the agreed scope or explain what must wait.

## 05 · Reflect — Days 14–15 · 15–16 Oct + post-block

### Explain why. Show what happened.

Compare choices with outcomes. Apply your judgement to a new situation.

- **Day 14 — 15 Oct — Reconstruct the journey**: Explain which practices helped, what they cost and how AI affected the work. Use evidence to choose the next investment.
- **Day 15 — 16 Oct — Transfer your judgement**: A three-hour individual scenario exam: explain choices, predict consequences and justify actions. No AI; supplied references only.
- **After — Post-block — Make your own argument**: Analyse your decisions and observed outcomes, including alternatives and AI's effects. Use the instructor's confirmed submission arrangements.

## The essentials. (modal)

CS403BKK Software Engineering · Harbour.Space Bangkok.
15 sessions, three hours each. One existing business. Three teams.

### The work

Keep a room-booking system working while you change it. Follow the member's journey from finding a space to paying and entering. Investigate whether your design choices help, what they cost and when a simpler approach fits.

### Bring these skills

Run and modify Python, run its tests, use Docker Compose, and push changes with Git and GitHub.

### AI throughout. Understanding stays yours.

Use AI to help with work you understand. For unfamiliar work, ask it to guide you through the method. Explain the steps and check the result. The final exam is supervised, no-AI, with supplied references only.

### Your evidence counts

| Evidence | Weight |
|---|---|
| Independently deployable service · team | 10% |
| Booking rescheduling shipped · team | 15% |
| Contracts, decisions, specs and requirements · team | 15% |
| Individual contribution during the course | 25% |
| Day 15 scenario exam · individual | 15% |
| Post-block analysis · individual | 20% |

Five short team assignments reuse your project evidence. The post-block analysis is separate; submission arrangements come from the instructor.

### Share responsibility

Everyone develops, checks and explains. On-call rotates within published course hours. Review the evidence before claiming a change is merged, deployed or useful to a member.
